Privacy policy

hermes Agent is a private tool used by one person, its owner. It only accesses the owner's own Google account, with the owner's permission.

Google data it accesses

Gmail messages and settings, and Google Calendar events, in the owner's account.

How the data is used

Only to help the owner manage their own mail and calendar, such as summarizing messages, tracking deadlines, preparing drafts and adding events.

Where the data goes

Google sign-in credentials are stored on the owner's own computer. Notes the assistant makes for the owner are also kept in the owner's own accounts and devices.

When the assistant works on a message or event, that content is sent to the AI model provider that powers the assistant so it can be processed. Google data is not sold, is not used for advertising, and is not shared with anyone else except at the owner's direction.

Removing access

The owner can remove this app's access at any time from their Google Account permissions page.
